Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

This policy explains what BeduSocial, the in-house social media tool of Sporteology Global Private Limited ("we", "us"), reads from social media platforms, what we do with it and how to have it deleted.

Who uses BeduSocial

Only members of our team. We use it to manage our own brands' accounts: we do not offer it to the public or to clients, and nobody outside our team can sign in.

What BeduSocial reads

When one of our team members connects an account we manage (for example a Facebook Page, an Instagram professional account, a Threads profile, a YouTube channel, a TikTok, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Bluesky or Mastodon account, or a WordPress site), the platform gives BeduSocial an access key for that account. Depending on the permissions granted, BeduSocial then reads:

  • the account's name, ID and profile picture;
  • the posts, photos and videos we publish, and their statistics (for example reach, views, likes, comments and follower counts);
  • comments, replies, mentions and direct messages that people send to our accounts, so that our team can read and answer them in one place;
  • public information about public business accounts (for example follower counts and recent public posts) that we compare our accounts with.

BeduSocial does not read the private data of people who interact with our accounts beyond what they send to those accounts (their public name, profile picture and the comment or message itself).

How we use it

  • to publish and schedule our own posts;
  • to show our team statistics and reports about our own accounts;
  • to read, answer, hide or moderate comments and messages sent to our accounts.

We do not sell this information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers or other companies. Captions and hashtags are suggested by an AI model that runs on our own computer; nothing is sent to outside AI services.

Where it is kept

On a computer owned by Sporteology Global Private Limited in India. Access keys are stored encrypted, every team member signs in with two-step verification, and backups are encrypted. BeduSocial only sends data to the platform each account belongs to (for example when it publishes a post).

How long we keep it

Access keys are deleted as soon as an account is disconnected. Statistics, comments and messages are kept for as long as we need them for our reports and replies, and are deleted on request (see Data deletion).

Google and YouTube

BeduSocial's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. BeduSocial uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove BeduSocial's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by writing to sporteologyglobalpvtltd@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

Children

BeduSocial is a business tool for our team and is not meant for children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
