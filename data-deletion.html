Data deletion

Last updated 1 November 2026

You can have the data BeduSocial holds about you deleted at any time.

If you commented on, messaged or mentioned one of our accounts

Email sporteologyglobalpvtltd@gmail.com with the platform and your account name or a link to your profile. We delete what BeduSocial holds about you (your comments and messages, your name and picture) within 30 days and reply to confirm.

If you connected an account to BeduSocial

  • In BeduSocial: disconnect the account in Settings. Its access key is deleted straight away.
  • On Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites) → remove BeduSocial.
  • On Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites → remove BeduSocial.
  • On Threads: Settings → Account → Website permissions → remove BeduSocial.
  • On Google and YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions → remove BeduSocial.

Removing BeduSocial on the platform stops all access at once. To have the statistics, comments and messages already saved for that account deleted as well, email us as above.
